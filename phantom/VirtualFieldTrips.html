Assignment Description

This assignment is for starting to create a depository of virtual field trips for future use in the classroom. These virtual field trips are great because they give the experience of a normal field trip without the hassle and costs of transporting students around.

VFT 1 - The Met

The Met, or The Metropolitan Museum of Art, is an art museum based in New York City and has an online interactive map that students can look through and explore different artifacts from history. It was chosen because of how interactive it is, giving students the chance to explore and see the things they are interested in. To use in the classroom, students can be given time to explore as an art or history lesson since those are not typically a key focus.

VFT 2 - Callisto: Space Innovation Tour

This tour is of the Orion spacecraft, where students can learn about the inner workings of the technology that flew to the moon. It was chosen because lots of students love the idea of space and astronauts. In the classroom, it can be done either synchronously or asynchronously with either one 50-minute session or two 25-minute sessions. I would probably have it be a sort of class reward, like some teachers would do with a movie day.

VFT 3 - National Aquarium

This tour is of the National Aquarium in Baltimore. It allows students to walk through the aquarium and also look at pictures and fun facts of the animals in the exhibits. It was also chosen because of how students often enjoy learning about animals, especially aquatic ones. It can be used similar to either of the previous tours, as part of a lesson, in students' free time, or as a reward.
