Assignment Description

This assignment is for beginning to create a depository of curated videos that can be used in the classroom.

The Penniless Pilgrim Riddle

This video is from TED-Ed, a website for teachers to expand their lessons with TED videos. The video is a math riddle that requires the viewer to think critically and help the pilgrim get to the temple with a net score of zero. I chose this video because of how well the riddle, as well as the solution, is explained, making it easy for viewers to understand. In the classroom, I would use video riddles like this one at the beginning of class to get students thinking critically. And because riddles are complicated, students will often make mistakes, and this will help to show them that it is okay to do so.

Drumming in Fractions

This video is from The Futures Channel. It is about how there are fractions in drumming (and all instruments). I picked this video because of how it makes fractions important to something outside of math; it can make the student care about them more. For use in the classroom, I would play it when students ask the dreaded "When will we ever use this?" question. This is a great way to make them care and understand that these concepts can be important to them.

News Quiz

This video is from PBS LearningMedia. This video specifically is a news-esque report from January 30 to February 5, although I would use the most up-to-date one. I chose this video series because of how it takes small news things from the week and turns them into a fun video that asks questions of the students, making them interact. I would use this in the classroom at the end of the school day on Friday as a nice way to end out the week.

Benji, the Bad Day, and Me

This video is from TeacherTube. The video is a read-aloud of the book, Benji, the Bad Day, and Me. I chose this video because of how it demonstrates emotional intelligence, empathy, and autistic traits (in the author's notes at the end). It goes through how the boy has had a bad day and that these emotions are okay; it also talks about how his little brother, Benji, is given some special treatment by their mother. At the end of the story, Benji sees that his brother is crying and helps him feel better the same way that their mother does for him. In the classroom I would use this video at the end of class as a way to remind students that it's okay to be sad and to be empathetic to others who are sad.

Native American History Preview

This video is from the Crash Course channel on YouTube. The video is an intro to a whole playlist of videos about Native American history. I chose it because of how it smoothly introduces the topic and hints at what is coming later. In the classroom, I would use this video (and the whole playlist) throughout a unit about Native American history. Playing each video before discussing topics to ensure that students have a base understanding of what is going to be discussed.
